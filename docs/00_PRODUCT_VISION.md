# Product Vision

## Working name

**LoomLogic Front Desk OS**  
Product codename: **Resolve**

## One-sentence definition

LoomLogic is an AI front-desk and revenue-recovery operating system that answers or recovers missed calls, manages customer conversations, books appointments, enforces follow-up commitments, and proves the outcomes it created.

## Core customer

Any appointment-driven or lead-driven business that loses revenue when the front desk cannot answer or complete a customer request.

Initial examples:

- automotive service and dealerships;
- home services;
- dental and wellness clinics;
- salons and spas;
- veterinary practices;
- legal intake;
- property services;
- repair businesses;
- professional services.

The MVP remains vertical-agnostic at the platform level. Industry templates may be introduced after the core system works.

## Problem

Traditional phone systems record calls and voicemails but do not resolve the underlying business intent. Basic AI receptionists often answer questions or book available slots, then stop when the call ends.

Businesses lose opportunities because:

- staff miss or abandon calls;
- transfers fail;
- after-hours calls go unanswered;
- requested times are unavailable;
- voicemails are not returned;
- callbacks are promised but forgotten;
- quotes and intake requests stall;
- cancellations create unused capacity;
- customer context is spread across channels;
- owners cannot distinguish activity from recovered revenue.

## Product promise

**Turn missed calls into booked business.**

LoomLogic should continue working until an opportunity is:

- booked;
- resolved;
- awaiting a known customer action;
- assigned to a human with a deadline;
- explicitly disqualified;
- opted out;
- or marked lost with a reason.

## Core product loop

1. A customer calls, texts, emails, or requests a callback.
2. LoomLogic identifies the person, intent, urgency, and desired outcome.
3. The AI answers, qualifies, books, routes, or collects structured information.
4. LoomLogic creates or updates a unified customer record.
5. If the desired outcome is incomplete, LoomLogic creates a Recovery Case.
6. Recovery Autopilot selects an authorized next action.
7. The customer or staff member is contacted through the appropriate channel.
8. The system tracks promises, deadlines, appointments, and status.
9. The case closes only when a verifiable outcome exists.
10. Analytics attribute estimated, confirmed, or verified recovered value.

## Primary differentiation

### Recovery Cases

A durable business object representing revenue or service at risk. It survives beyond a call or message.

### Recovered Revenue Ledger

An auditable chain from the original missed opportunity through the actions and final outcome.

Attribution levels:

- `estimated`: based on configured service or opportunity value;
- `confirmed`: booking or service completion confirmed;
- `verified`: payment, invoice, or authoritative external system confirms value.

### Promise Tracker

Extracts commitments such as callbacks, quotes, documents, and confirmations; assigns ownership and deadlines; escalates missed promises.

### Booking Rescue

When the requested slot is unavailable, LoomLogic searches alternatives, locations, employees, waitlists, and future cancellations instead of ending the interaction.

### AI Failure Recovery

Detects tool failure, misunderstanding, repeated corrections, caller frustration, failed transfer, or disconnected calls and starts a human-safe recovery process.

### Operations Copilot

An internal assistant that can search, summarize, draft, and execute explicitly authorized actions through a secure tool gateway.

## What the product is not

- It is not a generic sales CRM.
- It is not merely a call-log viewer.
- It is not a prompt editor wrapped around ElevenLabs.
- It is not a full PBX replacement in the first release.
- It is not a banking platform in the MVP.
- It is not a medical diagnostic product.
- It is not an autonomous agent with unrestricted database access.

## Success metrics

North-star metric:

**Verified or confirmed customer outcomes recovered from previously unresolved interactions.**

Supporting metrics:

- missed calls recovered;
- time to first response;
- booking conversion;
- percentage of Recovery Cases resolved;
- callback promise completion;
- cancellation slots refilled;
- repeat-call reduction;
- transfer failure recovery;
- estimated, confirmed, and verified recovered value;
- AI automation rate;
- human escalation rate;
- customer opt-out and complaint rate;
- integration/tool failure rate.

## Product principles

1. Outcomes over activity.
2. Reliability over novelty.
3. Human control over unrestricted autonomy.
4. Tenant isolation by construction.
5. Explainable and auditable actions.
6. Self-serve setup with progressive power.
7. Integrations behind replaceable adapters.
8. A polished interface that communicates trust.
9. Portable data and architecture.
10. Build the smallest end-to-end slice that proves value.
