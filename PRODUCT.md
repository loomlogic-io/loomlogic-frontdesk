# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are business owners, front-desk managers, and front-desk team members at
appointment-driven or lead-driven businesses. They use LoomLogic while triaging unresolved
customer requests, coordinating follow-up, and verifying that promised outcomes happen.
Customers interact with the service through communication channels, but the authenticated
product surface is designed for the business team.

## Product Purpose

LoomLogic Resolve turns missed or incomplete customer interactions into durable, owned
outcomes. Success means an opportunity is booked, resolved, awaiting a known action,
assigned to a human with a deadline, explicitly disqualified, opted out, or marked lost
with a reason. The system must make recovered outcomes and accountability clearer than raw
call activity.

## Positioning

LoomLogic continues working after the call ends until the underlying business outcome is
resolved and auditable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and trustworthy. The interface should communicate operational confidence
without sounding clinical, flashy, or artificially intelligent.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic admin template, a neon AI dashboard, a glassmorphism experiment,
or a marketing site forced into an operations product. Avoid oversized card collections,
decorative motion, novelty interaction patterns, and visual claims unsupported by real
data.

## Design Principles

1. Put unresolved work and next actions before vanity activity.
2. Make authorization, ownership, and system state legible.
3. Use familiar product patterns so the interface disappears into the task.
4. Show proposals, approvals, and completed actions as distinct states.
5. Prefer calm density and reliable feedback over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must be keyboard operable, use semantic markup and
visible focus, avoid color-only meaning, support reduced motion, and remain usable on
mobile for triage. Dates, times, currency, phone numbers, and language must be adaptable
to the organization locale and time zone.
